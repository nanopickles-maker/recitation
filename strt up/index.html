A poem for the becoming

The Long Way Up

A breath between each step.

every morning the mountain waits, patient as a held breath. It does not ask how fast I climb, only that I climb at all.

I stumble, and the stones remember. I rest, and the wind forgives. Falling is not the end of the road, only the road teaching me its shape.

The summit was never the promise. The promise was the one who kept going.

02 The dream beside me

Every morning the mountain waits, patient as a held breath. It does not ask how fast I climb, only that I climb at all.

My dream did not arrive with trumpets. It came quietly, folded small, and asked me to walk beside it, so I walked.

I stumble, and the stones remember. I rest, and the wind forgives. Falling is not the end of the road, only the road teaching me its shape.

Some evenings the path disappears, and the clouds hide the top from me. But I build the road again, one small step at a time.

The summit was never the promise. The promise was the one who kept going. So if you are tired, unsure, and small, take the step. The mountain is waiting.
